# Content Rating & Theme

## Content Rating: Mature (PEGI 16 / ESRB M)

### Rating Justification
- **Violence**: Bloody battles and combat are permitted
- **Strategic Warfare**: Territorial conflicts and raids
- **Resource Competition**: Aggressive economic gameplay
- **Social Dynamics**: Competitive multiplayer interactions

### Regional Considerations
- **Gore Toggle**: Client setting to reduce/remove blood effects
- **Cultural Sensitivity**: Content adapted for different regions
- **Age Verification**: Appropriate age checks for mature content
- **Parental Controls**: Options for younger players

## Visual Theme: Realistic

### Art Direction
- **Map-First Design**: Real-world geography as primary visual element
- **Grounded Aesthetics**: Believable, non-fantasy visual style
- **Clean UI**: Minimal, functional interface design
- **Photorealistic Elements**: Real-world textures and materials

### Visual Hierarchy
1. **Real-World Map**: Primary visual focus
2. **Settlement Overlays**: Clear, readable building icons
3. **UI Elements**: Subtle, non-intrusive interface
4. **Effects**: Meaningful, not distracting visual feedback

## Tone & Atmosphere

### Realistic Approach
- **Believable World**: Grounded in real-world geography
- **Logical Progression**: Technology and advancement make sense
- **Realistic Consequences**: Actions have believable outcomes
- **Authentic Feel**: Players feel like real-world strategists

### Mature Themes
- **Territorial Control**: Realistic power dynamics
- **Resource Scarcity**: Economic competition and conflict
- **Strategic Depth**: Complex decision-making
- **Social Dynamics**: Realistic player interactions
